    HOW TO READ THE ARENA

    One map. Comparable movement.

    1. 01
      Territory is return-only

      The larger territory always has the higher signed return for the selected view. Every asset keeps at least five percent of the map.

    2. 02
      Only ROUND resets

      ROUND resets at the canonical battle-start candle. 1H–1W are rolling market context and are never used as the active battle scoreboard.

      The live ROUND map uses Binance boundary data for immediate feedback. The allowlisted StudioNet deployment independently evaluates the five-exchange policy; only its FINALIZED result controls claims.

    4. 04
      Freshness stays visible

      Crypto trades 24/7, but a feed can still pause or carry a prior quote. The arena shows when each price was genuinely updated instead of treating every packet as new.
